# Den privacy policy

Den is a chat in which devices connect directly to each other (a peer-to-peer network).

**Identity.** Your identity (key, name, avatar, status) is created and stays on your device. Den has no accounts and asks for no email or phone number.

**Messages and photos** go only to people you add yourself: friends and members of your caves. Connections are encrypted. So that messages reach people who are offline, encrypted copies of conversations are kept on our server (a blind peer). The server has no keys and cannot read them. Its space is limited: when it runs out, it removes copies.

**Your device's IP address** is visible to the people you connect to and to the STUN servers (Cloudflare, Google) that help set up voice connections, as with any network app.

**We don't collect** analytics, crash reports or advertising data. Den shows no ads.

**The camera** is used only to scan QR codes and to take photos you choose to send.

**Deleting your data.** In settings choose "Delete identity from this device", or uninstall the app: this removes everything from the phone. Messages you sent stay with the people who received them. More: [Deleting your data](../delete/).
