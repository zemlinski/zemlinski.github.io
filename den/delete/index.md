# Den – deleting your data

Den has no accounts on a server. Your identity, friends, caves and messages are stored on your device.

**To delete everything:**

1. Open Den → settings.
2. Choose "Delete identity from this device" and confirm.

Uninstalling the app also removes all its data from the phone.

**What stays:**

- messages you sent, with the people who received them;
- encrypted copies of conversations on our server. The server has no keys and cannot read them, and it removes copies when it runs short of space.
